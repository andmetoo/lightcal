# Community rules

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and personal attacks are not.

Use issues and PRs for project work. Respect privacy and credit others' contributions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed, explaining decisions when safe and appropriate.

Contact repository owner [@andmetoo](https://github.com/andmetoo) for moderation concerns through an available contact method on their profile. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. A dedicated confidential moderation contact has not yet been established.
